Viktor Moskvoretskii

EPFL

vvmoskvoretskii@gmail.com

I am a Fellowship PhD Student in Computer Science at EPFL DLab. I am now working on LLM safety under the supervision of Prof. Robert West. We are largely targeting the pre-training phase for safety, reflected in the Model Raising paradigm.

My research interests are Personas inside of the LLM and their connections to safety and general LLM behavior.

I have previously worked as a Research Lead at Skoltech NLP Lab focusing on QA and uncertainty estimation.

I have pursued my M.S. in Machine Learning at HSE University under the supervision of Prof. Chris Biemann and Irina Nikishina.


I am open to collaborations and to supervising student projects; see the collaboration page.
