Viktor Moskvoretskii

EPFL

vvmoskvoretskii@gmail.com

I am a Fellowship PhD Student in Computer Scince at EPFL DLab. I am now working on pre-training LLMs to make them safe and empathetic under the supervision of Prof. Robert West

My research interests also include trustworthiness, uncertainty quantification and inducing LLM self-correction

I have previously worked as a Research Lead at Skoltech NLP Lab focusing on QA and uncertainty estimation.

I have pursued my M.S. in Machine Learning at HSE University under the supervision of Prof. Chris Biemann and Irina Nikishina.
